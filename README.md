# Sendity

**An email platform built around Trust, Control, and Simplicity.**

> **Powerful when you need it. Invisible when you don't.**

---

## Who Sendity Is For

Sendity is for people who want more confidence in their email communications without sacrificing simplicity.

Whether you're sending a personal message, working with clients, sharing important documents, or managing communication within a team, Sendity is designed to adapt to the purpose of each message.

Some people will simply want to send email.

Others may want additional visibility, reusable templates, communication policies, or document controls.

Sendity doesn't assume every message should be treated the same.

Instead, it gives the sender the choice.


---


## Why Sendity?

Email is one of the world's most important forms of communication.

Yet once we press **Send**, we often lose visibility into what happens next.

Was the message delivered?

Was it opened?

Could the recipient access the attachment?

Did their email client silently remove or block part of the message?

Sending an email shouldn't feel like dropping a letter into a mailbox and hoping for the best.

Sendity was created to explore a different approach.

Sendity helps people create, send, and understand their email communications with greater clarity.

From simple everyday messages to more intentional communications, Sendity provides tools that help senders understand what happens after an email is sent.

One where email remains simple by default, but can also provide meaningful insights if they're needed.

Rather than making decisions on behalf of the sender, Sendity puts those decisions back where they belong—with the people communicating.

## Core Capabilities



### Email

At its core, Sendity is an email platform designed to keep communication simple.

Send messages, manage conversations, and communicate with confidence through an experience built around clarity and control.

Additional capabilities are available when needed, but sending an email should always remain straightforward.



### Templates

Create consistent communications without starting from scratch every time.

Sendity provides reusable templates for common messages while allowing people to create and share their own designs.

Templates help save time, maintain consistency, and make everyday communication easier.


### Meaningful Insights

Once an email is sent, it's natural to wonder what happened next.

Sendity provides optional insights that help people better understand their communications after they are sent.

Whether it's confirming that a message was opened, understanding how it was received, or reviewing communication history, the choice always remains with the sender.

Meaningful insights are designed to inform—not to intrude.


### Communication Policies

Not every message serves the same purpose.

Sendity allows people to choose how their communications should be handled based on what matters for each message.

Whether it's adding visibility, applying additional controls, or defining how a communication should behave, the sender remains in control.

Communication Policies help make sure each message is treated according to its purpose.


### Document Control

Important documents sometimes need more than simply being attached to an email.

Sendity provides optional controls that help people share documents with greater confidence while keeping the sender in control.

Whether it's protecting sensitive information, managing access, or understanding document activity, Document Control provides additional options when they are needed.

Simple sharing remains simple. Additional control is available when required.

### Audit History

Important communications may need to be understood after they are sent.

Sendity keeps a record of communication activity, helping people review what happened and maintain confidence in their messages over time.

Whether reviewing a previous message, checking communication events, or understanding a document's history, Audit History provides clarity when looking back is important.

A clear history helps people stay informed about their communications.

## Self-hosted or Cloud

Sendity is designed to give people a choice in how they use it.

Whether you prefer to run everything on your own infrastructure or simply want to sign in and get started, the experience should feel familiar.

### Self-hosted

Run Sendity on your own infrastructure with complete control over your environment, your data, and your deployment.

Ideal for individuals, organisations, and teams that prefer to manage their own systems.

### Cloud

Use Sendity without managing servers or infrastructure.

Sign in, create your account, and start communicating while we take care of the platform.


Whichever option you choose, the goal remains the same:

**Trust. Control. Simplicity.**
